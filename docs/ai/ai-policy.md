# AI Use Policy

## Purpose

Team 03 uses AI as an engineering **copilot**. AI may assist with thinking, development, testing, review, debugging, and documentation, but human engineers remain responsible for all work accepted into the repository.

## Core Principle

AI-generated output is treated as a suggestion or draft, not as authoritative engineering work.

The team member using AI must understand, review, verify, and be able to explain any AI-assisted work they contribute.

## Permitted Uses

AI may assist with:

* brainstorming and comparing approaches;
* requirements and edge-case analysis;
* explaining technical concepts;
* generating or reviewing draft code;
* debugging and refactoring;
* generating or reviewing tests;
* architecture discussions;
* identifying risks or defects;
* drafting technical documentation; and
* reviewing engineering work.

## Unacceptable Uses

The team will not:

* submit AI-generated work that no team member understands;
* trust AI output without human review;
* fabricate requirements, tests, results, citations, or engineering evidence;
* claim verification occurred when it did not;
* use AI to bypass peer review or required team participation; or
* provide passwords, API keys, tokens, private records, or other protected information to AI systems.

## Human Review and Verification

Before AI-assisted work is accepted, the responsible team member must:

1. understand the output;
2. review it for correctness;
3. check it against project requirements and constraints;
4. modify or reject incorrect or unsuitable suggestions;
5. verify important behavior using appropriate engineering evidence; and
6. be able to explain the final result.

Verification may include testing, code review, documentation review, manual inspection, or comparison with authoritative technical documentation.

Higher-risk work requires stronger verification.

## AI Use Logging

Meaningful AI-assisted engineering work will be recorded in:

`/docs/ai/ai-use-log.md`

AI use should be logged when it materially affects:

* requirements or planning;
* architecture or engineering decisions;
* implementation;
* testing;
* debugging that results in a change;
* review;
* documentation; or
* phase-gate evidence.

Minor spelling, formatting, or simple syntax assistance does not need to be individually logged.

## Engineering Decisions

AI may suggest solutions or alternatives, but final engineering decisions remain team decisions.

Significant AI-assisted decisions should remain traceable through appropriate issues, pull requests, tests, decision records, or other repository evidence.

## Handling Incorrect AI Output

If AI-assisted output is incorrect or unsupported, the team will correct or reject it, determine whether related work was affected, and repeat appropriate verification.

## Team Expectations

* AI is a copilot, not a replacement for the engineer.
* Humans remain responsible for all submitted work.
* AI-assisted work must be understood and reviewed.
* Verification should match the risk of the work.
* Significant AI use must remain traceable.
* AI does not replace peer review, testing, or engineering judgment.
