## Team Purpose

Our goal is to operate as a collaborative engineering team while building Campus Connect in a way that is organized, maintainable, and reviewable.

We want all team members to understand the project as a whole rather than working only within isolated responsibilities. Engineering decisions, implementation, testing, documentation, and AI-assisted work should remain visible and understandable to the team.

## Roles and Shared Ownership

Primary engineering roles are maintained in:

`docs/team/roles.md`

Current specialized roles are:

* **Team Lead:** Carlos
* **Planning & Process Lead:** Sherry
* **Architecture & Development Lead:** Brendan
* **Quality & Review Lead:** Sanjana
* **Operations & Evidence Leads:** Jaylen and Dominic

Every member is also a Developer.

Primary ownership means responsibility for keeping an area organized and review-ready. It does not mean that only the primary owner may work in that area.

The team will reduce knowledge silos through pull-request review, shared documentation, discussion of important engineering decisions, and backup role ownership.

## Communication

The team will use **Microsoft Teams** as the primary communication channel for day-to-day project communication.

**GitHub Issues** will be used to document bugs, technical tasks, blockers, and other work that should remain visible and traceable within the repository.

Team members should respond to normal project communication within a reasonable period based on the urgency of the request.

Urgent blockers, deadlines, or problems affecting other team members should be communicated through Microsoft Teams as soon as possible.

Important engineering decisions should not exist only in private messages. Relevant outcomes should be documented in GitHub when appropriate.

## Meetings and Coordination

The team will meet regularly on:

**Wednesdays at 1:00 PM**

Meetings will focus on:

* current progress;
* blockers and bugs;
* upcoming work;
* important engineering decisions;
* task ownership;
* assignment or phase-gate readiness; and
* action items.

Important decisions and action items should be recorded in repository evidence, GitHub Issues, or meeting notes when appropriate.

## Decision Making

Normal engineering decisions should be discussed by the team members involved in the affected work.

The team should consider requirements, technical constraints, testing, maintainability, and project scope when making decisions.

When agreement cannot be reached, the relevant specialized role owner should help organize the discussion. Significant unresolved decisions may be brought to the Team Lead and, when necessary, the instructor.

Major technical decisions should be documented when they materially affect the project.

## Participation and Accountability

Team members are expected to:

* contribute consistently to project work;
* complete agreed responsibilities;
* communicate blockers early;
* participate in review when needed;
* keep assigned engineering evidence current;
* keep relevant GitHub Issues updated; and
* use their own GitHub identity when contributing.

If a team member cannot complete an agreed responsibility, they should communicate this to the team as early as possible so the work can be adjusted or reassigned.

## Conflict and Escalation

Disagreements should first be addressed directly and respectfully within the team.

For missed commitments or participation problems, the team should:

1. communicate directly with the affected member;
2. clarify expectations and blockers;
3. adjust responsibilities when reasonable; and
4. document significant role or ownership changes when necessary.

The instructor should be involved when repeated communication attempts fail, participation problems continue, or the issue cannot reasonably be resolved within the team.

## AI and Engineering Responsibility

Team 03 uses AI as an engineering **copilot**.

AI may assist with brainstorming, requirements, implementation, debugging, testing, review, and documentation. AI does not replace human engineering responsibility.

Team members must understand, review, verify, and be able to explain AI-assisted work before it is accepted into the project.

Meaningful AI-assisted work will be recorded according to:

`docs/ai/ai-policy.md`

and

`docs/ai/ai-use-log.md`

## Team Commitments

The team agrees to:

* communicate clearly and respectfully;
* keep project work visible and traceable;
* use issues, branches, pull requests, and reviews appropriately;
* document bugs and technical work through GitHub Issues;
* communicate blockers before they become larger problems;
* review important work rather than merging without oversight;
* keep documentation current as engineering work occurs;
* use AI responsibly as a copilot; and
* take responsibility for work contributed to the repository.

## Charter Review

The team will review this charter when team responsibilities, communication practices, meeting schedules, or project needs significantly change.

The charter may be updated throughout the semester as the team learns what working practices are most effective.
